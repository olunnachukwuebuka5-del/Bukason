Automatic gathering

The server gathers by itself, every 3 minutes until 10,000 entries, then about once an hour until 30,000, then it stops. When the queue runs low it thinks up new topics across many subjects (science, history, health basics, technology, culture, money, food, sports and more). Questions that repeat a saved one in different words are skipped. Change the numbers with AUTO_FAST_UNTIL and AUTO_ENTRY_LIMIT in Netlify, or use Pause.
